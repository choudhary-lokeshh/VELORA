# RTC provider eligibility

## Status and method

Research date: **2026-08-20**. Sources below are current official product, developer, legal, and terms materials retrieved on that date. This is technical and product eligibility research, not legal advice and not procurement approval.

No provider is selected, approved, or production-eligible. Public silence about VELORA's adult social and dating business is treated as **unapproved**, and a policy page that cannot be retrieved is treated as unread rather than as permissive. Marketing pages, sandbox access, a free tier, and a passing local adapter test are not approval. Production requires written provider confirmation naming VELORA's business model, the exact call product, participant age assurance, launch countries, media regions, data residency, retention, IP-address handling, callback authentication, revocation behaviour, and restricted-content posture.

The candidate set covers the shapes an architecture can take rather than every vendor: two managed WebRTC platforms with room semantics (LiveKit Cloud, Daily), three carrier-scale platforms (Twilio Video, Agora, Vonage Video), two infrastructure-level offerings (Cloudflare Realtime, AWS Chime SDK), and two self-operated options (self-hosted LiveKit, coturn as TURN-only). No further candidate has enough differentiated official evidence to justify expanding the set before launch countries and the recording decision exist.

## What the matrix decides

Nothing is selected. Two candidates are removed on evidence rather than on absent paperwork — Agora by its own published prohibition, and the Cloudflare SFU by an app-global track namespace that gives a one-to-one call no provider-enforced boundary. Two cannot be assessed at all until their terms are readable. The remainder are unresolved on the same written-confirmation gap the identity providers carry.

The technical requirements the matrix was scored against, restated so a later candidate is assessed the same way:

- server-side session creation with a real isolation boundary between one call and every other;
- participant-scoped, session-scoped, capability-scoped, short-lived join credentials that are not room secrets;
- server-side participant revocation and session termination, with a stated latency;
- raw-body-authenticated events, or an equally strong authenticated channel, with duplicate and replay tolerance assumed;
- current-state retrieval for reconciliation, independent of event delivery;
- ephemeral TURN credentials, never static ones;
- Web and native mobile client support under the surfaces VELORA actually ships;
- documented media regions, IP-address handling, logging, and retention;
- recording off by default and not silently enableable.

## Required provider decision record

Before any real adapter or vendor SDK enters the repository, a provider-specific ADR and signed review must record:

- legal entity, account, environment separation, and the exact product and plan;
- VELORA's business description and written approval for adults-only social and dating voice and video, including any future mature-content relationship actually in scope;
- launch countries, media regions, residency, and the provider's own transit and relay geography;
- controller and processor roles, subprocessors, transfer mechanism, breach terms, audit rights, and law-enforcement disclosure;
- what the provider logs, for how long, and whether participant IP addresses, SDP, or ICE candidates appear in any dashboard, export, or support channel;
- join-credential scope, minimum and maximum TTL, revocation mechanism, revocation latency, and behaviour of a credential presented after revocation;
- event authentication scheme, event identity, ordering and duplicate semantics, retry and abandonment behaviour, and account and environment binding;
- participant removal and session termination APIs, their idempotency, and their observable effect;
- recording and transcription defaults, and proof that neither can be enabled by configuration VELORA does not control;
- rate limits, concurrency limits, quotas, and the behaviour at each ceiling;
- support and escalation, incident ownership, status transparency, sandbox isolation, secret rotation, and migration and exit terms.

## Review triggers

These findings are dated. Re-verify before any decision rests on them if the retrieval date has aged past six months, if a vendor publishes new acceptable-use or supplemental terms, if a product's lifecycle status changes, or if VELORA's launch countries or content scope change. A stale entry supports no decision.

## Current decision

`DEFER UNTIL PROVIDER INTEGRATION`: no RTC provider, hosting mode, or relay design selected. `DECISION REQUIRED`: operations ownership for any self-operated option. `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`: recording, transcription, regional availability, retention, and emergency-calling posture.
