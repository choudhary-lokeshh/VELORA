# Identity verification provider eligibility

## Status and method

Research date: **2026-08-18**. Sources below are current official product, developer, privacy, and terms materials. This is technical/product eligibility research, not legal advice or procurement approval.

No provider is selected, approved, or production-eligible. Public silence about VELORA's adult social, creator, depicted-person, mature-content, or commercial use is treated as **unapproved**. Marketing claims and document-country counts do not establish country/legal eligibility. Production needs written provider confirmation naming VELORA's business model, exact purpose, actor age, launch country, collection channel, verification method, data role, residency, retention/deletion, biometrics, sub-processors, callback security, and restricted-content posture.

The candidate set deliberately includes identity specialists (Persona, Veriff, Entrust/Onfido, Jumio, Incode), a broad compliance platform (Sumsub), a payments-adjacent option (Stripe Identity), and an age/identity specialist (Yoti). No additional candidate has enough differentiated official evidence to justify expanding the set before requirements and launch countries are approved.

## Required provider decision record

Before any real adapter or SDK enters the repository, a provider-specific ADR and signed review must record:

- legal entity/account/environment and exact product/workflow;
- VELORA business description and written approval for adult social, creator verification, depicted-person evidence, commercial KYC, and any mature-content relationship actually in scope;
- launch countries, subject residence/document country, platform/browser/mobile support, accessibility, and alternative path;
- controller/processor roles, subprocessors, transfer mechanism, storage/processing region, breach terms, audit rights, government/law-enforcement disclosure, and model/fraud-network use;
- data collected/returned, biometric method, consent/notices, raw-media access, manual review, bias/accuracy evidence, and prohibited attributes;
- retention defaults/minima/maxima, configurable deletion/redaction, backups, legal hold, account termination, data-subject request routing, and proof of deletion;
- hosted-link lifetime/resume/binding, server idempotency, retrieval by idempotency/reference, callback signature/authentication, replay behavior, event identity/order/retry, account/environment binding, cancellation, expiry, outage, and reconciliation;
- pricing/rate limits, support/escalation, incident ownership, sandbox isolation, secret rotation, migration/exit/export, and provider shutdown behavior.

Silence, a sales demo, sandbox access, or a passing local adapter test is not approval.

## Current decision

`DEFER UNTIL PROVIDER INTEGRATION`: no provider/model/workflow selected. `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`: all live jurisdiction, privacy, biometric, retention, residency, age, KYC, creator, depicted-person, and mature-content uses.
